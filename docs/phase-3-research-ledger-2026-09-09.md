# Phase 3 research ledger

Research was performed September 8–9, 2026 with exact queries in Google using the in-app browser. Google localized results to Surat, India and displayed AI Overviews. The results below are evidence of what was observed, not a claim about US ranking or search volume.

The user approved allowing a guide when an AI Overview exists if the guide adds substantial sourced analysis, a worked comparison and a practical worksheet. The eight new guides follow that rule. The five excluded calculator publishers from the objective were not observed in the first-page results for the accepted topics.

| Topic | Observation | Added value used |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Space heater monthly cost | Overview supplied a generic $35–$65 range, a 1,500 W table and the basic formula. | Separate rate, schedule and thermostat duty cycle using the calculator's exact inputs, with CPSC safety guidance. |
| Dehumidifier IEF and cost | Overview explained a generic 24/7 range but did not explain the official IEF metric. | Connect IEF's L/kWh laboratory definition to measured kWh, off-cycle energy and duty-cycle input. |
| Electric water heater use | Overview supplied broad geyser wattages and an India rupee example. | Separate equivalent element runtime from measured tank consumption and avoid claiming tank-hours as universal. |
| Window AC running cost | Overview supplied generic BTU/watt ranges and a formula. | Use a retrieved Frigidaire model-specific 670 W input and show compressor duty and BTU-versus-watts distinction. |
| Standby device cost | Overview supplied generic household ranges and device wattages. | Use a measurement worksheet, continuous versus scheduled hours, and explicit scenario arithmetic. |
| Tile waste and spare boxes | Overview supplied generic 10–20% allowances. | Keep cutting allowance, repair reserve and box rounding as separate decisions, using Daltile's attic-stock guidance. |
| Refrigerator versus EnergyGuide | Overview omitted the label's national-price assumption and a numerical price-versus-consumption reconciliation. | Hold kWh constant while changing rate, then hold rate constant while changing consumption. |
| Vaulted ceiling paint | Overview used broad footprint multipliers and average heights. | Calculate right-triangle slope length, gable rectangles/triangles and separate wall/ceiling products. |

## Retrieved sources used

- EIA Electric Power Monthly table 5.6.A: https://www.eia.gov/electricity/monthly/epm_table_grapher.php?t=table_5_06_a. June 2026 US residential price 18.34 cents/kWh, released August 26, 2026.
- FTC EnergyGuide consumer guidance: https://consumer.ftc.gov/articles/how-use-energyguide-label-shop-home-appliances. Labels use typical use and national average prices; actual use and local energy prices vary.
- ENERGY STAR refrigerator guidance: https://www.energystar.gov/products/refrigerators. Size, seals, airflow and heat-source guidance.
- ENERGY STAR dehumidifier criteria: https://www.energystar.gov/products/dehumidifiers/key_efficiency_criteria. IEF definition and thresholds.
- ENERGY STAR dehumidifier testing: https://www.energystar.gov/products/dehumidifier_testing_and_capacity. IEF includes off-cycle energy and newer 65°F portable-unit test conditions.
- Frigidaire model support: https://www.frigidaire.ca/Owner-Centre/Product-Support/fhww083wbe. FHWW083WBE 8,000 BTU model lists 670 W cooling input.
- CPSC heater safety: https://www.cpsc.gov/node/66106. Three-foot clearance guidance. Direct wall outlet guidance was also retrieved at the CPSC release URL during research, but that release URL returned an unavailable response during final link verification.
- Willmar Municipal Utilities appliance reference: https://wmu.willmar.mn.us/energy-programs/energy-calculator/. Estimated reference watts used by the Phase 2 preset list.
- Daltile tile FAQ: https://www.daltile.com/how-to/faqs. Approximately 10% attic-stock recommendation and dye-lot context.
- BEHR ULTRA technical sheet: https://s7d9.scene7.com/is/content/behr/5558_tds_uspdf. March 2026 coverage 250–400 sq ft/US gallon and spray-loss limitation.
- DOE appliance-estimation PDF copy hosted by BPI: https://bpi.org/__cms/docs/Estimating-Appliance-and-Home-Electronic-Energy-Use-_-Department-of-Energy.pdf. Nameplate and monitoring guidance.

All numerical examples in the eight pages are either calculations from these retrieved facts or explicitly identified scenario inputs. No manufacturer wattage was generalized across a product class.
